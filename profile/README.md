#### 1. Kya BlueStacks PC ke liye safe hai?

Haan, BlueStacks 100% safe aur legal emulator hai. Isme koi malware ya virus nahi hota jab tak aap ise official website se download karein.

#### 2. Kya BlueStacks chalane ke liye Graphics Card zaroori hai?

Nahi, Integrated graphics (jaise Intel HD Graphics) par bhi yeh chal jata hai, lekin heavy games (jaise Free Fire ya PUBG) ke liye dedicated Graphics Card recommend kiya jata hai.

#### 3. BlueStacks slow chale toh kya karein?
